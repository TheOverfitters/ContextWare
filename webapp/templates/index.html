About ContextWare

ContextWare was developed by The Overfitters to bridge a critical gap in software engineering analytics: understanding the true contextual intent behind code changes

Our Goal: To leverage Large Language Models (LLMs) to automatically classify commits, parse maintenance lifecycles, and extract rich contextual metadata directly from a repository's history.

By simultaneously cross-validating outputs across multiple models, ContextWare ensures high-fidelity, bias-resistant insights. This empowers teams to reduce technical debt, track software evolution accurately, and improve long-term project maintainability
